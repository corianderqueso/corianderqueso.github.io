Not-so-commonly asked questions

Who are you?

I am not going to answer that! The whole point of this is to stay anonymous. If you do figure out who I am, please be quite about it.

Why a website?

While most people draw, write, or do any other hobby as a mean of self expression, I like to code. This offers an outlet for me to be creative in a way that I really enjoy.

Why not show your friends?

I want to have a freedom to talk about whatever I please and not fear judgement from others that I know. Also, its fun to rant and is why I made Scream Into the Void so others had a place to type and type and type without consequence.

What is your gender and sexuality?

I am a cis male and a closeted bisexual. Ive told a lot of friends but never anything public. I suppose this is public but you get the idea

How did you make this?

I use Neovim with many plugins managed by Lazy.nvim as my code editor, I test locally using the Node.js https server, I create assests with the GNU Image Manipulation Program, I run Linux Mint on my laptop, and I use GitHub pages for deployment. I did not use any front-end frameworks, just raw HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Additionally, nothing has been generated with AI, however I do use the AI Overview in Google for refrence when it is convienent.
